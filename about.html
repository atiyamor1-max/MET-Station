Our Mission

Using the station we aim to provide clear, real-time weather information in the Kinneret subdistcrit area. We harness our knoledge in the electronic field to use modern sensors and a clean visual interface. The goal is to make environmental data accessible and easy to understand for all users.

How the System Works

The system collects real-time measurements such as temperature, humidity, wind speed, and light intensity. These values are processed and displayed in this very site on the "current" page in a dashboard-inspired.
